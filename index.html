How it works & our terms

  • Notifications: your case manager posts every update — filed, under review, approved, paid — visible in your Activity feed.
  • Getting your cash: the holding agency pays you directly to your linked bank account, debit card, Zelle, external transfer, or wire — or to your assigned beneficiary, a bill payment, a donation, or your investments.
  • Transfer restrictions: transfers under $10,000 are reviewed with your bank before release (usually within 24 hours). Transfers of $10,000 or more are held for 3 business days, then transferred or cancelled.
  • Source of funds: payouts always come from the official holding agency — never from TrueClaim directly.
  • Your data: your information is used only to file and track your claim.
  • No upfront fees: we never ask for payment to release your funds.

Understand your claim

Where the money comes from
Banks, employers, utilities, insurers and stores report unclaimed funds to state and federal offices every year — refunds, wages, bonds and balances that were never collected.
How your payout is protected
Every payout is verified against official records and sent by the holding agency directly to your chosen method — it never passes through this portal.
What happens next
Your case manager files the claim and posts every update to your Activity feed. Your balance stays live here until you choose to cash out.
